Building a version

Read docs/DEMO_API.md for the exact contract, then copy the 20-line skeleton into your own versions/vN-slug.html. Import only ../src/rig.js and ../src/console.js (plus their stylesheets), keep everything else inside your file, and serve the repo root over HTTP: python3 -m http.server 8000. Slug directions above are suggestions, not assignments of taste: rename yours if you take it somewhere better.
